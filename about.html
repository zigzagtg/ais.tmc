About Us

Capturing stories. Sharing moments. Representing AIS

WHO WE ARE

Every story deserves to be told.

Media Club is a student-led club at Akosombo International School that focuses on storytelling through photography and journalism. We document school life, highlight student achievements, and bring events to life through visual and written media.

OUR ACTIVITIES

What We Do

Members of Media Club learn the fundamentals of photography, journalism, and digital media. We cover school events, interview students and staff, and create engaging content that represents the vibrant AIS community both on campus and online.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision

Our vision is to be the voice and lens of Akosombo International School. We aim to creatively capture, document, and share the life of the school through photography and journalism, while developing students’ media skills and promoting AIS and its activities with authenticity, responsibility, and pride.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Approach
